# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participate with respect, patience, and technical honesty. Welcome questions,
different experience levels, and evidence that changes an earlier conclusion.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not
accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository security contact or to
an OpenHoo organization owner. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions,
limit participation, or ban accounts when needed to protect contributors.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid
unnecessary disclosure about reporters or affected people.
